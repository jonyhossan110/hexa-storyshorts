# Style C — Paint Explainer

Reference image: `../examples/free-apps-storyboard-style-c-paint.png` ("Every Free App Has a Price"). Study its deliberate roughness: wobbly mouse-drawn lines, big-head stick figures, hand-drawn arrows and labels. That roughness is the style — do not polish it.

## Why this style (evidence)

Stick-figure/Paint explainers are a proven breakout format: The Paint Explainer reached ~2.02M subscribers with roughly 1.9M average views per video (top video ~13M), and new clone channels passed 60–90K subscribers within months of launching in 2026. It is also the **cheapest and fastest** style in this skill to produce: the script carries the video; the drawing only has to be clear and funny.

## Look

- Crude MS Paint drawing: wobbly hand-drawn lines, flat fill colors, white background.
- Stick figures with oversized round heads and big simple expressions.
- Hand-drawn arrows, circles, underlines and short labels inside the frame are welcome (they are part of the charm) — but the caption bar still carries the beat's message.
- Simple props drawn as icons: a phone outline, a sack labeled DATA, a building labeled BIG COMPANY. Labels inside the art are allowed in this style only, kept to 1–3 words.
- Color is flat and sparse: mostly black lines, a few fill colors (green FREE burst, brown sacks, one red pin). No gradients, no shading, no backgrounds beyond a ground line.

## Series logic — "Every X…"

The strongest Paint format is a repeatable claim: **"Every [thing] is [secretly doing Y]"** — Every Free App Has a Price · Every Password Rule Is Wrong · Every Public Wi-Fi Wants One Thing. One mechanism per video, proven with the drawing, fixed in Beat 4. Keep the same stick character as the recurring "everyman".

## Master prompt (storyboard)

```
One single storyboard image: a 2x2 grid of four vertical 9:16 phone-video frames drawn in a crude MS Paint style: wobbly hand-drawn mouse lines, flat fill colors, white background, funny stick figures with big heads, hand-drawn arrows and underlines. Very large bold caption bars at the bottom of each frame: black bar, white capital letters. [FRAME 1 — SHOCK: ... Caption: '...'] [FRAME 2 — CAUSE: ... Caption: '...'] [FRAME 3 — MECHANISM: ... Caption: '...'] [FRAME 4 — PAYOFF: ... Caption: '...'] Deliberately rough and charming Paint look, no other text anywhere except short hand-drawn labels inside the art.
```

## Master prompt (single scene)

```
[SCENE VISUAL], crude MS Paint drawing style, wobbly hand-drawn mouse lines, flat fill colors on a clean white background, funny stick figures with big round heads, hand-drawn arrows and labels, deliberately rough and charming, vertical 9:16
```

## Rules

- Rough on purpose: if a generation comes out polished, regenerate — polish kills this style.
- The everyman stick character stays identical (same head, same smile) in every frame and every video.
- One idea per frame, drawn as literally as possible (data = sacks, sold = money changing hands).
- Humor comes from the drawing's honesty, never from crude jokes at anyone's expense.
- Captions follow the house rules in `script-formula.md` (≤5 words, ALL CAPS, black bar).
