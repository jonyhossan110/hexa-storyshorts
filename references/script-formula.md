# Script formula — the 4-beat story system

The whole skill stands on this page. It translates a proven faceless-explainer formula (one contrarian mechanism, an underdog identity, an instantly recognisable visual identity) into short tech/life stories.

## The 4 beats (always this order)

| Beat | Job | Time in a ~50 s Short | What the viewer feels |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — SHOCK | The threat/secret is ALREADY happening | 0–10 s | "Wait — that's me?" |
| 2 — CAUSE | The neglected thing that makes it possible | 10–22 s | "Oh no, I do that" |
| 3 — MECHANISM | The one hidden secret, named + highlighted | 22–38 s | "So THAT's how it works" |
| 4 — PAYOFF | The fix/twist; threat defeated, hero relieved | 38–52 s | "I can do that" + share impulse |

Why shock first: the old way (calm setup → facts) loses the viewer in 2 seconds. Opening on the threat already inside the house gives the rest of the video a question to answer: *how did he get in — and how do I get him out?*

## Beat-writing rules

- **Beat 1 (Shock):** show the end state of the danger, not its beginning. A stranger already using the Wi-Fi; the vault already open; the data already flowing out. Present tense. No "did you know".
- **Beat 2 (Cause):** one concrete neglected object or habit — dusty, old, unchanged, ignored. Give it a number when you honestly can ("3 YEARS", "1 password", "7 apps"). Specific numbers drive curiosity; invented numbers are forbidden.
- **Beat 3 (Mechanism):** the single secret the video exists to reveal. It gets the visual highlight (Style A: the red circle; Style B: the spotlight/close-up). Name it in the caption in plain words ("PASSWORD STILL admin"). If a video seems to need two mechanisms, it is two videos.
- **Beat 4 (Payoff):** the fix must be as simple as the threat was scary — one action, visibly done, threat visibly gone (broken connection line, red X, kicked-out stranger), hero visibly happy (thumbs up, shield icon). End the narration with the identity line when it fits: the viewer is the kind of person who now knows the secret.

## Steps inside the beats

Beats are the story's chapters; STEPS are what the viewer actually sees. Break each beat into steps — **one step per narration sentence or distinct action** — and give every step its own image and its own moment on screen. A beat with two sentences is two steps. The video must read as a walk through steps: the image changes exactly when the story moves. Never let one image sit on screen while the narration describes two different actions.

## Captions

- One caption bar per beat (per scene), **ALL CAPS, max 5 words**, white bold text on a black bar at the bottom of the frame.
- The 4 captions alone, read in order, must tell the whole story. Test: read only the captions — if the story is unclear, rewrite the beats, not the captions.
- Numbers and the mechanism word are the caption's payload; cut every filler word ("REALLY", "JUST", "ACTUALLY") unless it changes the meaning.

Worked example (the router story):
`A STRANGER USES YOUR WI-FI` → `NEVER TOUCHED IN 3 YEARS` → `PASSWORD STILL admin` → `ONE RESTART KICKS HIM OUT`

## Narration

- Calm storytelling voice, plain words, short sentences. One idea per sentence.
- 1–2 sentences per beat (~25–35 words total per beat max). Write for the ear: no brackets, no abbreviations, no lists.
- Never open with "Hey guys" or the channel name. The first narration line lands on the shock frame within the first second.

## Titles

Formula: **counter-intuitive claim + (comparison OR named mechanism)**.

- "Hackers Don't Break In. They Log In." (claim + mechanism)
- "The Router Nobody Touches" (object + neglect)
- "Your Selfie Is a Map" (object + hidden mechanism)
- "Why Billionaires Borrow Money They Don't Need?" (claim + paradox)

Rules: the title is the Beat-3 secret, teased — never fully given away; no word the video does not prove; no emoji in the title unless the channel's established style uses them.

## Series logic (how one video becomes twenty)

One mechanism supports many angles. When a video works, do not change topic — re-angle the same mechanism:

- Router mechanism ("the default password never changed") → the same story for the CCTV camera, the smart TV, the office printer, the old email account.
- Each new video keeps the same archetypes and style; only the object and the room change.

And one identity supports a channel: every video is made for one named viewer type ("people who never touch their router", "skinny guys", "anyone starting from zero"). Say who it is for in the planning line before scripting.

## Fact discipline

- Every number, claim and "most people" statement must be true and checkable. If you cannot verify it, drop the number and keep the story.
- Security/safety topics: the payoff must be the genuinely correct fix, not a dramatic one.
- No fear-bait: the threat shown must be a real, common one.
